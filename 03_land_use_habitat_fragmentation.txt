TOPIC: Land Use ↔ Habitat Fragmentation

PURPOSE
This knowledge entry supports multi-metric reasoning between land use/land cover and biodiversity.

ENVIRONMENTAL VARIABLES
Relevant land variables include:
- Land use
- Land cover
- Crop type

Relevant biodiversity variables include:
- Biodiversity indicators
- Species richness
- Habitat diversity

CHALLENGE REQUIREMENT
The challenge explicitly identifies:
Land use ↔ habitat fragmentation

The AI Environmental Scientist should consider land-use information together with biodiversity information when generating environmental reasoning.

Land-use and land-cover information should be considered alongside biodiversity indicators.

Examples of relevant input fields include:
