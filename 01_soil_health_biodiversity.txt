TOPIC: Soil Health ↔ Biodiversity

PURPOSE
This knowledge entry supports multi-metric reasoning between soil health and biodiversity.

ENVIRONMENTAL VARIABLES
- Soil pH
- Soil organic carbon
- Soil moisture
- Biodiversity indicators
- Species richness
- Habitat diversity

CHALLENGE REQUIREMENT
The challenge explicitly identifies:
Soil health ↔ biodiversity

The AI Environmental Scientist should not treat soil health and biodiversity as completely separate variables. When relevant information is available, the system should consider soil-health variables together with biodiversity indicators.

MULTI-METRIC REASONING
Relevant soil-health variables include:
- pH
- Organic carbon
- Moisture

Relevant biodiversity indicators include:
- Species richness
- Habitat diversity

When these variables are provided, the system should explain how they are being considered together before generating a recommendation.
